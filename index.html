Educational use only

This app supports education and revision in clinical numeracy. It does not provide clinical advice and must not be used to calculate, verify or make decisions about real patient care or medicine administration. Always follow local policy, approved medicines information and supervision requirements.

    About Clinical Numeracy Learning

    Clinical Numeracy Learning is a free, independent educational resource for nursing and healthcare learners. It supports practice with calculations in patient case scenarios.

    Educational use

    This app supports education and revision in clinical numeracy. It does not provide clinical advice and must not be used to calculate, verify or make decisions about real patient care or medicine administration. Always follow local policy, approved medicines information and supervision requirements.

    Privacy

    The app does not collect, transmit or share personal data. Progress is stored only in your browser/device using local storage and can be cleared with Reset progress.

    NEWS2 reference

    NEWS2 content is presented for educational revision. Use current Royal College of Physicians NEWS2 resources and your local escalation policy in practice.
